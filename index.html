Welcome to N.Café

N.Café is a unique café experience in Battambang inspired by the simplicity and peaceful life. Located in Chrab veab, we offer a peaceful escape from the busy city — a place where you can enjoy quality coffee and refreshing drinks.


As one of the peaceful café in the city, N.Café blends greenery, open space, and a welcoming atmosphere to create more than just a coffee stop — it's a place to unwind, connect, and enjoy the moment.


Whether you're meeting friends, working remotely, or just taking a break, N.Café is your go-to destination for comfort, flavor, and calm.
